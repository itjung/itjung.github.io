Openings

The group welcomes inquiries from motivated undergraduate students, graduate students, and postdoctoral researchers interested in early-universe science, JWST data analysis, Lyman-alpha emission, galaxy evolution, and machine-learning methods for astronomy.

Please send a short note describing your interests, research experience, and the kind of project you would like to pursue.
